ABOUT THE TEAM

Young heroes, guided by experienced mentors.

Young Justice is a group of young heroes connected to the Justice League. Members are trained and guided by experienced heroes while developing teamwork, discipline, responsibility, and leadership.

The Team specializes in covert operations, while training includes physical and intellectual development. Not every member needs superpowers to contribute.

Good training is also about judgment, teamwork, responsibility, and learning.
